About

RAK Center for Outdoor Comfort

RAK Center for Outdoor Comfort (RAKCOC), a joint initiative between RAK Municipality and AURAK, focuses on practical applied research on outdoor comfort in the Middle East region.

The Center supports adopting outdoor thermal comfort solutions in Ras Al Khaimah and the region through modeling, testing, implementation, design advisory, guidelines, education, and field research.

The Center also plans to expand into micro-climate management, climate change adaptation, outdoor landscape design, urban heat island reduction, and Nature Based Solutions.

Vision

To become a leading regional center for understanding microclimatic characteristics in outdoor urban spaces and improving the comfort of people using them.

Mission

To understand outdoor thermal conditions through research, user feedback, instruments, modeling, and long-term monitoring.
